CLAWD BAND 1.0.2 - FEATURES
===========================

THE BAND
- A row above the prompt box in the Claude desktop app: clock and session
  stats on the left, the sky and Clawd on the right.
- In a terminal, a small block-character Clawd instead.

CLOCK
- Pixel desk clock showing the time, ticking over each minute.
- Glows blue instead of orange at night.

SESSION STATS
- 5H and 7D: your 5-hour and 7-day usage limits as glowing bars with percents.
    0-40%    green
    40-70%   ink (black, or light at night)
    70-90%   yellow
    90-95%   red
    95-100%  red, pulsing
- SESSION: what this session has cost so far.
- CTX: how full the context window is, as a bar split by category
  (messages blue, system tools orange, MCP tools green, other grey, buffer pale).
  Estimated before the first reply so it always shows.
- HIT: the prompt cache hit rate of the last reply.
- TOK/S: output speed of the last reply.
- CACHE: minutes left before the prompt cache goes cold (1 hour after a reply).
    Frost creeps over the digits in the last 10 minutes; frozen at 0.
    Greyed out while a turn is running (the turn refreshes it anyway).
- STATUS: one word for what Claude is doing, a dot, and the turn's elapsed time.
- Stats update live during a turn (at most every 4 seconds) and on every reply.
- Every chip shows from the start (dashes until measured) and keeps a fixed
  width, so the band never jumps around.
- Limits and each session's last reply, hit rate and speed are remembered, so
  a resumed session opens with them instead of blanks.

STATUS WORDS
- While working: Thinking, Replying, Drafting, Compacting, Reading, Editing,
  Writing, Running, Searching, Finding, Fetching, Browsing, Delegating,
  Spawning, Asking, Planning, Remembering, Publishing, Scheduling, Messaging,
  Watching, Loading tools, Wrapping up, Using.
- While idle, by time of day:
    Dawn         Stirring
    Morning      Brewing
    Late morning Ready
    Midday       Lunching
    Afternoon    Lounging
    Golden hour  Basking
    Dusk         Unwinding
    Evening      Drowsy
    Night        Sleeping

CLAWD'S FORMS
- Idle while waiting; LookAround every random 10-20 seconds by day.
- Thinking from the moment you send a message and between tools.
- One form per tool while it runs:
    Typing      Edit, Write, NotebookEdit
    Computer    Bash, PowerShell, Monitor
    Searching / Busy (picked at random)   Read, Grep, Glob, and any other tool
    Fetching    WebSearch, WebFetch, browser tools
    Delegating  Agent, SendMessage, start_session
    Asking      AskUserQuestion
    Skill       Skill
- Oops when a tool call errors.
- Finishing a turn, looped whole for at least 10 seconds:
    Done     a normal turn
    BigDone  a turn over 60 seconds
    Sad      an interrupted turn
- Asleep (dimmed) at night.

THE SKY BY DAY (05:30 - 23:15, eight periods of 2h13m)
- Each period tints the band and draws its own sky beside Clawd.
- The sun crosses one arc: rising out of a haze low on the left at dawn,
  highest at midday, sinking into haze low on the right at dusk.
- Fuzzy clouds drift through, lit for the hour, fading at the edges.
    Dawn         05:30  peach      pink cloud, last stars fading, hot-air balloon rising
    Morning      07:44  cream      white cloud, two birds, paper plane looping
    Late morning 09:57  near-white clouds at two depths, kite on a string Clawd holds
    Midday       12:10  default    plane with a fading contrail, 3D monarch butterfly
    Afternoon    14:23  yellowish  three fat clouds, dandelion seeds drifting
    Golden hour  16:36  amber      gold-lit cloud, a V of five geese heading home
    Dusk         18:49  orange     rose cloud, first stars, sky lanterns rising
    Evening      21:02  indigo     full moon low on the left, stars, two z's

THE SKY BY NIGHT (23:15 - 05:30)
- The band goes dark whatever the system theme; stats and Clawd dim.
- Glowing crescent moon, twinkling four-point stars, three z's drifting up.
- Comets with glowing heads, dusty foggy tails and faint ion tails, gliding
  top-left to bottom-right on random passes, fading out at the edges.

COMMANDS
- /keepwarm: re-sends the conversation so the prompt cache stays warm another
  hour. Plays a fire glow on CACHE, or a thaw if it was frosting over.
  Declines if the cache has already gone cold.
- /ctxdebug: temporary; prints what the session reports about context.
